The problem LintelDesk tries to solve

Many productivity tools are designed around cloud services and recurring subscriptions. LintelDesk takes a different approach by combining several desktop tools into one Windows application and keeping day-to-day files local.

LintelDesk combines four functions — Docs, Sheets, Tasks, and Canvas — into one Windows program. The application is designed to work with files stored on your computer and is sold with a one-time license rather than a monthly subscription. It is intended primarily for people who work on a Windows PC and prefer a local workflow. It does not currently provide real-time multi-user collaboration or mobile applications.

How LintelDesk handles your data

Clear information about where your files are stored and how the application handles data.

STORAGE

Local files only

Workspaces are saved to your computer in the .linteldesk workspace format, with support for formats such as .md, .csv, .json, and .png for individual documents and exports.

NETWORK

No account required to use it

LintelDesk does not require an online account or sign-in to open and edit your files. An internet connection is used for purchasing and downloading the software; normal use is designed to work offline.

TELEMETRY

No usage tracking

The application is designed not to send usage analytics or document content to a server during normal use. See the Privacy Policy for the specific data practices and limitations.

BACKUPS

You are responsible for backups

Because files are stored locally, LintelDesk does not provide a built-in cloud backup service. We recommend keeping your workspace folder in a backup routine that works for you, such as an external drive or a trusted backup service.
